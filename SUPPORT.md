# Support

- Something is wrong, or you want something changed: open an issue in
  [optiflume/feedback](https://github.com/optiflume/feedback/issues/new/choose).
- Questions about how OptiFlume works in your tool: the docs at https://optiflume.ai/docs, then
  [Discussions](https://github.com/optiflume/feedback/discussions).
- Your account, subscription or invoice: https://optiflume.ai/support (private).
- Security: security@optiflume.ai, never a public issue.

Before reporting a bug, run `optiflume doctor --bundle` and attach the file it saves to your Desktop;
it holds no prompts, code, or names of your folders and files (https://optiflume.ai/docs/troubleshooting).
